Woodland: broadleaved, coniferous and mixed woodland, plantations, copses.
Arable: cultivated farmland, crop fields, tilled ground, horticulture.
Improved grassland: managed farm grassland, reseeded or fertilised pasture, grass leys, silage and hay fields.
Semi-natural grassland: rough or unimproved grassland, chalk and limestone downland, neutral hay meadows, acid grassland, plus fen, marsh, swamp and reedbed.
Mountain, heath and bog: moorland, upland and lowland heath, blanket and raised bog, montane ground, bare mountain rock, crags and scree.
Saltwater: the open sea and inshore marine water, birds on or over the sea.
Freshwater: open water bodies, lakes, reservoirs, gravel pits, ponds, rivers, streams, canals.
Coastal: intertidal and shore zone, estuaries, mudflats, saltmarsh, sandy and shingle beaches, sand dunes, rocky shore.
Built-up: towns, cities, villages, suburbs, gardens, parks, industrial land.
